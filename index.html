🗺️ WHAT THE ICONS MEAN

🌽 Corn = fills your boost

🎁 Mystery Box = gives you a power-up

🛝 Ramp = jump forward

🚪 Shortcut Gate = bonus distance

🎃 Pumpkin / hazard = slows you down if you hit it

🪶 Feather Shield = blocks hits for a short time

Q or ITEM button = use your mystery item

📍 Mini-map = shows your race position

Steer with ← → or A / D • Press SPACE for corn boost • Press Q for your item • On iPad, use the big touch buttons.
